01 · Business intelligence dashboard

LA Stay Shortlist

USC Marshall, DSO 576 team project · Fall 2026

Decision supportedWhich Los Angeles neighborhoods and room types should a first-time visitor shortlist for a given nightly budget and stay length?

  • Interactive Streamlit dashboard with budget, stay-length and room-type filters, a neighborhood map, and distance to Downtown, the beach and LAX.
  • Head-to-head view compares two neighborhoods on price, share of listings within budget, and review history.
  • Clear data rules: 5,570 listings with no price stay in counts but are left out of price statistics (never imputed); extreme prices above Q3 + 3×IQR are flagged, not deleted.
  • A methods page explains how every number is calculated and states its limits, such as listed nightly rates excluding fees and taxes.

02 · Product strategy & customer research

AfterChat: a coffee chat CRM

2026 USC Marshall Global MS Business Analytics Case Competition · Team Uno, 6 members

Business questionGraduate students network constantly but lose the value of each conversation. What should a follow-up tool actually do, and for whom?

  • Designed and analyzed a 60-person survey: 72% had a coffee chat in the past 3 months, and 38% struggle with timely follow-ups or knowing what to write.
  • Segmented users by networking frequency and chose active networkers (28%, three or more chats) as the early-adopter target.
  • Tied every feature to survey evidence: thank-you drafts, follow-up reminders, AI summaries, an advice library and job alerts linked to contacts.
  • Addressed adoption risks raised by respondents (AI accuracy 27%, privacy 15%) with a pilot, refine, expand rollout.
